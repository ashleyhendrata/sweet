## 1. Log in
1. Open the website link above on your phone.
2. Type the shared **email** and **password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**. You'll stay logged in — no need to do this every time.

## 2. Do a count (the daily job)
You'll land on the **Count** screen. Items are grouped by category.

- Tap a **category name** to open or close it.
- For each item, tap **–** or **+** to change the number.
- Or **tap the number** to type the exact amount (handy for big counts).
- **It saves by itself.** There is no save button. If something can't save you'll see a yellow warning at the top.
- Use the **Search** box at the top to jump straight to an item.

> Goal: a full count takes about 5 minutes. Go category by category.

## 3. What the red **REORDER** badge means 🔴
A red **REORDER** badge means that item is **at or below its reorder level — we're low, so it needs to be ordered.**

- The **banner at the top** tells you how many items need reordering right now (e.g. _"⚠ 6 items need reordering"_). Green means nothing is low. ✅
- Items with no reorder level set never show a badge — that's normal.

## 4. Print the order report 🖨️
1. Tap **Report** (top-right).
2. Tap **Print / Save as PDF**.
3. In the print box, turn **off** "Headers and footers" for a clean page.

- The report shows a **Needs Ordering** list first, then the full inventory.
- Want a **blank sheet to write counts on by hand?** Change **Layout → Blank checklist** before printing.

## Need to add or fix an item?
Tap **Manage** (top-right) to add, edit, archive, or reorder items and categories. **Archive** seasonal items rather than deleting them — deleting is permanent. When in doubt, ask your manager.
